Hello, I am Pooja. 

I'm a generalist who bridges the world business and technology. I care less about being labeled  "product manager" than about working on problems where the product decisions have downstream consequences for real people. I'm drawn to deep user research, data & feedback driven the decisions, and collaborating with diverse set of stakeholders around the globe. I am passionate about human collaboration and the technology impacting human experiences.

I have a background in computer science & technology management. I am comfortable having & making system design decisions. At the same time, I enjoy the challenge of aligning people with different and diverse opinions on a shared goal. 

Since I started building AI systems, I think carefully about what they do to the people who use them. That instinct shapes both my product work and the research I do outside it. 

I'm a visual storyteller whose primary medium is photography. I also enjoy creating through collage making, writing, and analog printmaking.

I am using this space to share the projects I have done, my learnings and meet people along the way.
