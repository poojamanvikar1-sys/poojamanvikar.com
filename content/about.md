I'm a Senior Product Manager with 11+ years building enterprise AI and platform products — most recently at Salesforce, where I led a GenAI agentic Slack platform serving 80,000+ employees across Workday, Concur, Okta, and CRM integrations.

I think carefully about what AI systems do to the people who use them. That instinct shapes both my product work and the research I do outside it.

## How I Got Here

My path has been less a ladder than a set of expanding circles.

I started at IBM India, building B2B e-commerce. Moved to Atlanta for a graduate degree in Information Systems at Georgia State. At Georgia-Pacific, I led a ServiceNow migration across 15 manufacturing sites and built a worker safety hazard reporting system that cut response times from 4 hours to 15 minutes and reduced injuries by 35% — the kind of outcome that reminds you why the work matters.

At Salesforce (2020–2025), I ran two integrated platforms:
- A unified employee support portal with semantic search, intent-based personalization, and 8.6M searches — deflecting 35% of support volume
- A GenAI agentic Slack platform: RAG architecture, multi-system orchestration, three-tier progressive resolution, multi-language support including Japanese. 75% organic adoption. 40% support automation.

I also made the $4.4M decision to discontinue Moveworks — a build-vs-buy analysis that required holding a room of stakeholders through an uncomfortable truth.

I was laid off in November 2025 in a Salesforce reduction in force.

## How I Think

I'm a generalist who bridges business and technology. I care less about being labeled an "AI PM" than about working on problems where the product decisions have downstream consequences for real people. I'm drawn to hard tradeoffs, honest post-mortems, and the places where the product roadmap and the ethics roadmap are the same document.

My background in documentary photography has probably shaped this more than anything formal. You learn to look at what's actually there, not what you expected to find.
